QuickBooks Hours App

Connection and reconnection information

QuickBooks Hours App connects to QuickBooks Online to retrieve authorized employee, project and worked-hours information for internal administrative and accounting purposes.

To connect or reconnect QuickBooks Online, an authorized user must sign in to the internal QuickBooks Hours App and select the option to connect with QuickBooks.

Only users authorized by Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados may initiate or renew the connection with the company’s QuickBooks Online account.

During the authorization process, the user will be redirected to Intuit to sign in and approve the requested access. After authorization, the user will return to the internal application.

For assistance, contact the system administrator of Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados.
