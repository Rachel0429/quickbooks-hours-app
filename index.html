About the application

QuickBooks Hours App is an internal business application developed for Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados.

The application allows authorized company personnel to consult employee, project and worked-hours information obtained from QuickBooks Online. It also supports administrative and accounting reports related to employee and project costs.

Access to the application is restricted to authorized internal users. QuickBooks information is accessed only after the corresponding company authorization process has been completed.
