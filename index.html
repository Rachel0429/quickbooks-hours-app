Welcome

QuickBooks Hours App is an internal web application developed for Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados.

The application allows authorized users to consult employee information, projects, worked hours, and cost reports by integrating QuickBooks Online with the company's MariaDB database.

This application is intended exclusively for internal administrative and accounting use.
