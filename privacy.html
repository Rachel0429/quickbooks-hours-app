Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

QuickBooks Hours App is an internal web application developed for Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados.

The application uses the QuickBooks Online API to retrieve employee, project and time activity information for internal reporting purposes.

Information obtained through QuickBooks is used exclusively for internal business operations. The application does not sell, rent or share personal or business information with third parties.

Authentication is performed using OAuth 2.0. Access credentials and tokens are handled securely and are only used to communicate with QuickBooks Online.

This application is intended solely for authorized personnel of Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados.
