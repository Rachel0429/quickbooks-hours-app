QuickBooks Connection Disconnected

QuickBooks Hours App

Disconnection information

The connection between QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Hours App has been disconnected.

After disconnection, the application will no longer be able to retrieve updated information from the connected QuickBooks Online company.

To reconnect the application, an authorized user must access the internal QuickBooks Hours App and complete the QuickBooks Online authorization process again.

For assistance with the disconnection or reconnection process, contact the system administrator of Ingenierías Jorge & Asociados.
